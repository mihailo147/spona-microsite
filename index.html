Getting started with Spona

Create a free account at https://sponasales.com/register. It comes with 3,000 credits, enough for three searches of 1,000 credits each. Open the chat and describe the customers you want: industry, company size, roles, countries, and anything that signals a good fit. The miners ask one or two follow-up questions, then show sample leads with the reason each one matches. If the samples look right, run the full search; the leads arrive in minutes with e-mail, LinkedIn and, on Standard and Deep Search, mobile numbers. Export to CSV or push to your CRM. No contract, setup or onboarding is needed.

Bulk, Standard and Deep Search

Bulk Search returns e-mail and LinkedIn for large lists at 1 to 9 credits per lead, with under 1% e-mail bounce. Standard Search adds verified mobile numbers at 3 to 55 credits. Deep Search scores every lead against your ideal customer profile, verifies it across its public digital footprint and enriches it through a waterfall of 40+ data sources, at 60 to 200 credits per lead, and explains why each lead matches. Use Bulk for volume outreach, Standard for calling, Deep for account-based work.

Where the data comes from

Spona combines its own database with 40+ data providers, queried in sequence until a verified e-mail or mobile number is found, and public sources such as company websites and professional profiles. Each lead is checked by The Verifier before export. Spona's accuracy target is 95%; if a delivery falls below it, report it through the help centre and the team investigates. Spona is GDPR- and CCPA-compliant; anyone can request removal of their data at https://sponasales.com/complaint-form?right=delete.

Exporting leads and connecting your CRM

Export any list as CSV, or push it to Salesforce, HubSpot or Zoho CRM. For sequences, Spona lists work with Salesloft, Lemlist, Outreach and Gong. Field names follow the standard contact schema (first name, last name, title, company, domain, e-mail, mobile, LinkedIn URL, location, ICP score, rationale).
